# Proprietary Systems Product Dioramas

## Selected direction

The v2 system uses clean 2.5D product dioramas. Each product is represented by one ownable hero object and one supporting product artifact rather than a generic icon inside a browser window.

## Competitive synthesis

- Google Workspace: truthful UI fragments, generous whitespace, light surfaces, and minimal decoration.
- Coinbase: a single bold physical object, strong silhouette, and controlled dimensionality.
- Odoo: differentiated app personalities across one family.
- Stripe: realistic product artifacts integrated with abstract color and depth.

The objective is not to copy any source. Proprietary Systems combines those qualities into a distinct navy, teal, and mint spatial system.

## Visual grammar

- Canvas: `320 × 240`, transparent.
- Camera: shallow axonometric or controlled three-quarter view.
- Light: top-left, consistent across every product.
- Depth: three layers maximum — base, hero, supporting artifact.
- Hero scale: 58–72% of the usable canvas.
- Stroke: `2–3px` navy on primary silhouettes only; interior details are mostly fill-defined.
- Corners: 10–18px on UI surfaces; circular objects use precise geometry.
- Shadows: one soft ambient shadow and flat offset/extruded side planes.
- Background: no full tile background; a mist halo or base plate may support the scene.

## Palette

- Navy `#0A2030` anchors every scene.
- Teal `#0097A7` carries product structure.
- Mint `#00E5C2` indicates action, success, or intelligence.
- Mist `#E6F2F2` and off-white `#F6FBFB` create quiet surfaces.
- One optional accent per product: cobalt, violet, coral, or amber.

## Semantic rules

- Show product behavior, not just category symbols.
- Use one dominant metaphor per product.
- Keep the silhouette legible at `160 × 120`.
- No embedded copy, logos, or tiny fake UI text.
- Peter.ai alone may use an agent character.
- Contracts and Signatures must remain visually distinct: agreement execution versus email identity.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The product is recognizable without its label.
2. The illustration remains clear at half size.
3. The visual center is balanced inside a 4:3 tile.
4. No accidental cropping occurs at the viewBox edge.
5. Paths are deliberate and editable; automatic tracing artifacts are prohibited.
6. SVGs contain no raster images, external URLs, fonts, or embedded text.
7. The set reads as one family without repeating the same composition.
