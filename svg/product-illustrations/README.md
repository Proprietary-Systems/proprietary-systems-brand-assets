# Proprietary Systems Product Illustrations

Native SVG illustrations for Proprietary Systems product tiles.

## Art direction

- Canvas: `320 × 240`, transparent.
- Geometry: rounded, front-facing 2D product scenes.
- Palette: Proprietary Systems navy, teal, mint, mist, and off-white.
- Accent colors are restrained and shared across the set.
- Strokes use round caps and joins at a consistent visual weight.
- No embedded text, external fonts, raster images, or remote dependencies.
