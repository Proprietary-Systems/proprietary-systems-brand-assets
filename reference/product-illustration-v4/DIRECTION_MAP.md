## Production rules

- One primary object per tile.
- Two dominant colors plus pale mist support.
- No isometric base, surrounding scene, tiny interface copy, or decorative halo.
- No more than one semantic modifier per product.
- Must remain recognizable at 48 × 48 and structurally intact at 24 × 24.
- Light and dark variants should preserve geometry and re-author contrast rather than invert colors.
- The direction sheet is raster exploration only; approved production assets must be reconstructed as deliberate native vectors or sourced from a consistently licensed professional family.
